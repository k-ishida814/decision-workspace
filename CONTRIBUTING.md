# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. This is a personal project, and I am not accepting pull requests.
Pull requests will be closed without review.

Issues are welcome:

- Bug reports: what you ran, what you expected, what happened, and `dw version`.
- Ideas and feature requests: what you are trying to do and why.

For security problems, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

You are free to fork the project under the [MIT license](LICENSE).
